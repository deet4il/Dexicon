# ⚡ SYNCDEX // Trainer OS
> *Created by DCA*

**SyncDex** is a unified daily productivity and reflection system designed with a retro Pokédex hardware aesthetic. It merges **Field Tasks** (a gamified quest checklist with a 1/16 Shiny spawn rate and persistent habit companions) with **Daily Resonance** (a contemplative journal locked to classical world wisdom and National Pokédex companions).

---

## ✨ Features

- **Field Tasks & Bounties:**
  - Create ad-hoc tasks organized by channels (`General`, `Work`, `Habits`, `Research`, or custom groups).
  - Every deployed bounty rolls a random wild Pokémon and a **1/16 Shiny encounter rate** with authentic shiny sprites and golden badges.
  - Check off a completed bounty to catch the specimen into your permanent Trainer's Box.
- **Daily Routines (Habit Locks):**
  - Toggle recurring daily habits that reset their checkboxes at midnight while keeping their companion Pokémon permanently locked.
- **Daily Resonance Journal:**
  - A locked daily quote drawn from global literature, Stoicism, Daoism, and classical philosophy.
  - Contemplative prompt sparks with a one-tap shortcut to inject thoughts into your log.
  - Automatic background saving with zero data leaving your device.
- **Trainer PC Box & World Codex:**
  - View all caught species, filter for shinies, and explore an infinite swipeable codex deck of world statements.
- **Multi-Format Export Suite:**
  - **JSON (`.json`)**: Machine-readable full state backup (tasks, habits, shiny box, and journal history).
  - **Word (`.doc`) & PDF (`.pdf`)**: Formatted archival reports listing completed daily bounties alongside your written reflections.
- **100% Offline Core**: Operates locally via `localStorage` with Service Worker caching for all Pokédex UI elements and sprites.
